# Day 1 — Threats and Telemetry

Preview lesson. Does not count as progress.

The CIA triad describes confidentiality, integrity and availability. Security threats include phishing, credential misuse, malware, denial of service and insider misuse.

A threat detection system needs observable evidence. Authentication logs can show login attempts, network flow metadata can show communication patterns, and process events can show host activity.

A burst of failed logins followed by success may merit investigation but does not by itself establish compromise.

Try this: map five threats to evidence sources and likely false positives. Use only synthetic or authorized data.

Review question: Why can't a dataset evaluate a threat for which it has no relevant evidence?
